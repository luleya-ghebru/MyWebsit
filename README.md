## Project Overview

LG Store is an independently owned retailer of branded LG electronics and home
appliances (TVs, fridges, washing machines, air conditioners, small
appliances), operating since 2010. This project builds LG Store a
professional website across three phases: HTML foundation (Part 1), CSS
styling and responsive design (Part 2), and JavaScript functionality, SEO and
deployment (Part 3).

**Mission:** To provide quality entertainment and household technology to
every household, backed by expert guidance and professional after-sales
services.

**Target audience:** Homeowners/families (28–55), renters furnishing on a
budget, tech-savvy researchers, small businesses buying in bulk, and existing
customers needing service/warranty support.

## Goals & Objectives

- Grow web traffic through a professional, optimised online presence
- Generate leads via enquiry forms, click-to-call and WhatsApp
- Provide product specs, pricing, warranty and service info online
- KPIs: 20% QoQ visitor growth, 2.5% conversion rate, under 45% bounce rate

## Key Features

- **Home** — hero banner, product categories, trust/social proof
- **About** — history, mission/vision, team, store locator
- **Products & Services** — TV/fridge/washer/aircon/appliance catalogue
- **Enquiry** — product, pricing, installation, repair and warranty form
- **Contact** — two store locations with maps, hours and contact form
