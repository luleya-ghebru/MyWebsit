LG Store Website
About the Project

This project is a website for LG Store, an independently owned retailer of LG electronics and home appliances.

The website allows customers to view products, learn about the store, make enquiries, and find contact and store location information.

Pages
Home – Introduction and featured product categories.
About Us – Store history, mission, vision, and team.
Products & Services – LG products, installation, repairs, and support.
Enquiry – Customer enquiry and service booking form.
Contact – Store locations, opening hours, maps, and contact form.
